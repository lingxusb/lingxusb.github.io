About

I am a researcher at the intersection of machine learning and biology. My work focuses on generative modeling of biological data and its applications in biodesign — using deep learning to decipher and engineer biological sequences across scales.

I am particularly interested in building long-context language models for genomes and plasmids, learning design principles for synthetic biology, and developing computational tools that accelerate the rational engineering of living systems.

Research Themes

Generative Genomics

Long-context language models that learn the grammar of DNA — from plasmids to whole bacteriophage genomes — and generate novel functional sequences.

Biodesign

Computational frameworks for rational design of genetic circuits, regulatory elements, and engineered organisms.

Quantitative Biology

Single-cell measurement, mathematical modeling, and ribosome profiling to dissect gene expression dynamics.
